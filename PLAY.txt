STRIDE / RUST3D MOVEMENT LAB
Version 0.6.0

GET GOING
Extract the ZIP, open Stride-Windows, and double-click Stride.exe.
No installer, Rust toolchain, extra downloads, or assets required.
Windows 10/11, 64-bit, with an OpenGL-capable graphics driver.

KEYBOARD / MOUSE
WASD or arrow keys     Move relative to the camera
Left or right Shift   Sprint (jumps keep full height and speed)
Space                 Tap hop / hold high jump / wall kick / rollout
Ctrl                  Crouch
Ctrl + Space          Long jump while moving
F                     Dive; press again during the slide to roll out
C                     Recenter camera
Q / E                 Orbit camera
Hold right mouse      Drag to look around
Mouse wheel           Zoom
Enter                 Confirm a menu selection
F1                    Controls and movement tips
F2                    Options (display, camera, controller, sound)
F11                   Fullscreen
Escape                Pause / back; choose Exit Game to quit

DEFAULT CONTROLLER (gameplay bindings can be changed)
Left stick            Analog walk/run; default 10 percent radial deadzone
Right stick           Orbit camera; default 10 percent radial deadzone
A / Cross             Jump / wall kick / rollout
X / Square            Dive; press again during the slide to roll out
RT / R2               Sprint
LT / L2               Crouch; add Jump while moving for a long jump
Y / Triangle          Recenter camera
Start / Menu          Pause / resume
Select / View         Controls
D-pad or left stick   Navigate menus; left/right changes options
A / Cross, B / Circle  Menu confirm, back (fixed regardless of gameplay bindings)
Controllers may connect after startup. Xbox-compatible controllers are the
primary target; other devices need compatible Windows drivers/mappings.

SAFE RESETS
Respawn at Checkpoint and Restart Course are pause-menu options.
Restart Course asks for confirmation, with Cancel selected by default.
No gameplay button resets the course. The former X/Square, R, and Enter reset
shortcuts are removed. Falling still returns you to your latest checkpoint.

DIVE AND ROLLOUT
Dive from the ground for a quick forward leap, or during a jump to reach farther.
You can dive once per flight. Landing transitions into a short belly slide.
Press Jump or Dive during that slide for a rollout hop with forward momentum.
A jump pressed shortly before landing is buffered. Holding Jump from an earlier
jump does not automatically roll out; release and press again.
With no further press, the slide recovers by itself. Hold Sprint to keep sliding.
Under a low ceiling you
stay low; use movement to crawl out before standing or rolling out.

HIGHLANDS / MOMENTUM IN VERSION 0.6
Esc / Start > Highlands / Momentum Routes > choose one of five regions.
1440 x 1680 m: sixteen times the previous map's area. Rolling hills, broad open
spaces, four mountains with peaks above 200 m, 180 additional blocks and 40 ramps.
Long Run has an open lane for chains. Rolling Basin tests hills and wide turns.
Ridge Descent starts high for downhill slides. Mountain Pass tests steep grades.
Block Fields provides varied blocks and ramps. See LANDSCAPE.md for the regions.
Travel resets free-run stats and sets your respawn point. Respawn in Pause returns
there. Return to Playground and Restart Course outside Skyway return to the lab.

Run with Sprint, long jump (Ctrl/LT + Jump), dive in flight, then press Jump on the landing slide
to roll out. Repeat to build speed. Each move adds less speed as you get faster,
but there is no hard horizontal speed cap. Watch SPEED, PEAK and CHAIN onscreen.
Downhill gravity adds speed; uphill spends it. Steep faces slide. Uphill jumps
keep upward slope momentum. Hold Sprint to extend a downhill slide and steer
it in gentle arcs. Faces too steep to stand on slide; jumps spring away from
them. Fast enough over a crest, you fly off it on a natural arc.
Strong forward input preserves earned speed; no input in the air coasts.
Use lighter stick or opposite input to brake; braking blends smoothly with how
far the stick is pushed. Fast turns need wider arcs.
See MOVEMENT.md for the rules, primary design references and tuning notes.

THE PLAYGROUND
Ramp to beacon 1, jump blocks to beacon 2, long jump to beacon 3, alternating
wall kicks to beacon 4, tunnel to beacon 5, thirteen spiral tower ledges to
beacon 6, and the broad ridge to beacon 7. Explore freely between timed runs.
The 360 x 420 m courtyard has eleven ramps, extra blocks, wall pairs and open space.
Beacons activate in order and save your respawn position. The run timer starts
when you move. Best times last for the current session.
Tap/hold jump changes height. Hold toward a wall in the air to slide down it,
then press Jump at any point to kick off. Alternate walls to climb. Standing
past a ledge with only your heel on it slips off unless you move back onto it.

SKYWAY / THE MOVING PLATFORM COURSE
Esc / Start > Skyway / Section Practice > Start Full Skyway Course.
Or walk up the broad entrance ramp to the east of the movement lab.
46 ordered components, 16 moving platforms, eight sections, a 44 m summit.
Amber moves; mint edges are fixed; violet decks save a checkpoint.
Follow the numbered targets and surface arrows. Dotted rails show motion paths.
Platforms pause briefly at both ends. Ride before jumping; lifts and shuttles
transfer their momentum into jumps. On narrower pads, slow down and aim centrally.
The early sections teach boarding, timing and lift exits. Later sections combine
diagonal rides, direct pad transfers, a long jump, low dive arch and summit climb.
See COURSE.md for section descriptions and technique hints.

Each section ends on a fixed checkpoint deck. A fall returns you there and you
replay that section. The retry count and timer continue. Respawn preserves motion
phases; Restart Course resets phases and the run, after confirmation.
Pause freezes platforms and the timer. Time counts real unpaused seconds, starting
with movement, jump or dive. Full-course best times last for this session.
Practice any section from the course menu; practice ends at its next checkpoint
and never enters the full-course best. Return to Playground is also a menu option.

OPTIONS
F2, or Options in the pause menu. Apply and Save commits your draft changes.
Default: 1280x720 render resolution, VSync on, 240 FPS safety cap, windowed.
VSync prevents tearing; changing it takes effect the next time the game starts.
Render sizes: 960x540, 1280x720, 1600x900, 1920x1080, 2560x1440.
Caps: 30, 60, 90, 120, 144, 165, 240 FPS, or uncapped.
Fullscreen scales the render image to the display and preserves aspect ratio.
Window size can differ due to desktop DPI. Actual FPS is shown onscreen.
Movement stays at 120 Hz. Rendering blends character/camera states between
physics updates; changing the cap does not change the movement constants.
Camera Settings: sensitivity, invert vertical look, automatic alignment.
Automatic alignment is off by default and pauses during manual camera input.
Controller Settings: separate stick deadzones (0-30%), jump/dive/sprint/recenter/
crouch bindings, vibration. Assigning an occupied button swaps the two actions.
Sound Settings: synthesized movement effects volume (0 mutes).
Preferences: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Rust3D\stride-options.cfg.
Existing 0.2 preferences remain compatible.

MOVEMENT FEEDBACK
Smoother camera obstruction handling and capsule corners, dive/roll animations,
landing squash, dust puffs, a landing-position marker and procedural movement
sounds. Supported controllers can vibrate briefly on movement events.
Sound/vibration availability depends on your output device and controller.
If no audio device is available, the game continues silently.

This prototype uses a variable-height capsule, heightfield terrain, blocks,
ramps and moving platforms.
Arbitrary meshes, an editor and music are future work.
The robot, level, shaders, font and movement effects are generated in code.

GRAPHICS / VERSION 0.5
Rounded block and robot edges, textured concrete and painted metal, roughness
highlights, per-pixel lighting, soft sun shadows and a gradient sky.
The seamless surface texture is embedded and generated at startup; no assets
need installation. Texture filtering reduces distant shimmer. Moving platforms
carry their texture with them. Course marker colors stay clearly visible.
Shadows follow the character and fade outside their local coverage area.
Bevels and surface relief affect rendering; gameplay dimensions stay the same.
If the higher graphics cost lowers FPS, choose a lower render resolution in F2.

TROUBLESHOOTING
If startup fails, update your GPU driver and look for stride-error.log
beside Stride.exe. The executable is unsigned.
