STRIDE / RUST3D MOVEMENT LAB
Version 0.2.0

GET GOING
Extract the ZIP, open Stride-Windows, and double-click Stride.exe.
No installer, Rust toolchain, extra downloads, or assets required.
Windows 10/11, 64-bit, with an OpenGL-capable graphics driver.

CONTROLS
WASD or arrow keys     Move relative to the camera
Left or right Shift   Sprint
Space                 Tap for a hop / hold for a high jump
Shift + Space         Long jump while moving at speed
Space against a wall  Wall kick (release and press again each time)
Q / E                 Orbit camera
Hold right mouse      Drag to look around
Mouse wheel           Zoom
R                     Respawn at the last beacon
Enter                 Restart course / confirm menu selection
F1                    Controls and movement tips
F2                    Options (resolution, frame cap, deadzone)
F11                   Fullscreen
Escape                Pause / back; choose Exit Game to quit

STANDARD CONTROLLER
Left stick            Analog walk/run, with a 10 percent radial deadzone
Right stick           Orbit camera, with the same deadzone
A / Cross             Jump, wall kick, or confirm menu selection
RT or B / Circle      Sprint; add A / Cross for a long jump
X / Square            Respawn
Y / Triangle          Recenter camera
Start / Menu          Pause / resume; choose Options in the pause menu
Select / View         Controls
D-pad or left stick   Navigate menus; left/right changes options
Controllers can be connected after startup. Xbox-compatible controllers are
the primary target; other devices need compatible Windows drivers/mappings.

THE PLAYGROUND
Run up the orange ramp to beacon 1. Jump the blocks to beacon 2.
Use the second ramp to reach the launch platform, then run and long jump
across the gap to beacon 3. Alternate wall kicks between the two tall
walls to reach beacon 4. Pass through the tunnel for beacon 5.
Then explore the much larger courtyard, climb the thirteen spiral ledges of
the tower in the northwest for beacon 6, and reach the broad ridge for beacon 7.
There are six ramps, extra jump blocks, wall pairs, and open running areas.
The course timer starts when you move. Beacons save your respawn position.
Falling returns you to your latest checkpoint. You can also explore freely.
Best times are kept for the current session.

REFINED MOVEMENT
Light stick input walks; full input runs. Stopping and reversing are quicker.
Tap jump for a low hop, or hold for a full jump. Air steering is more responsive.
Wall kicks require a fresh press in the first 120 milliseconds of contact.
There is 35 milliseconds of grace after leaving the wall, and a brief wall
slide. Alternate walls rather than hanging on one wall indefinitely.

DISPLAY OPTIONS
Default: 1280 x 720 render resolution, 60 FPS cap, windowed.
F2 opens Options directly. Change rows with left/right, then Apply and Save.
Resolutions: 960x540, 1280x720, 1600x900, 1920x1080, 2560x1440.
Caps: 30, 60, 90, 120, 144, 165, 240 FPS, or uncapped.
Deadzone: 0 to 30 percent in 5 percent steps; default is 10 percent.
Render resolution controls the game image. Fullscreen scales that image to
your display and preserves its aspect ratio. Window size can differ due to DPI.
Movement simulation stays at 120 Hz, independently of the rendered frame cap.
The HUD and Options show actual FPS, which can be below the selected cap.
Preferences are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Rust3D\stride-options.cfg.

This first prototype uses a box character collider and static blocks/wedges.
The robot, level, shaders, and font are procedural.
No audio, vibration, or controller rebinding yet.

TROUBLESHOOTING
If startup fails, update your GPU driver and look for stride-error.log
beside Stride.exe. The executable is unsigned.
